I'm Kate Lowry. I am a singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist, but primarily a bassist from Dallas, TX. I am a freshman at Berklee College of Music majoring in Bass Performance, and I'm super excited to see what I end up doing in this next chapter of my music education journey!

My music education ranges across all different genres. I grew up in the School of Rock program, and was a student there from 2015-2025. With them, I played a number of venues and shows including Rock in Rio Lisboa and Summerfest Milwaukee with my location's' "house band". I performed in seven years worth of themed sets ranging anywhere from 80's Pop to heavy metal's "The Big 4". I finished my time at the program, and took from them a very diverse range of rock and funk artists that would continue to expand with time.

For high school, I went to an arts magnet school called Booker T Washington High School for the Performing and Visual Arts. I heavily indulged myself in jazz, fusion and latin music throughout my four years there. I played a number of really cool school shows, and even got to perform at my high school graduation.

During high school, I formed my first band that actually recorded music. I'm part of a punk/metal power-trio called AKA. Being a part of AKA really taught me what it was like to write music as a band. We released our debut album in September of 2024, and each of the songs on our first record was carefully composed and arranged by the three of us.

Now that my musical journey is moving into a college setting, I'm wanting to take on as much music as possible. I'm part of a post-hardcore band called The Frontground. I'm always wanting to jam, I really enjoy backing songwriters in live settings as their bassist, and I hope to write a whole bunch of music with all the talented musicians I meet and have met on my journey!
